# Product Requirements Document

> Phase 2 · Owner: product-manager · Source: requirements/Ralysa_Spec.md (v0.3) + docs/market/summary.md (G1 approved 2026-09-25) · Last updated: 2026-09-25
> Status: **Draft for G2 review.** Market recommendations R-1 to R-9 and the "Proposed changes to the spec / roadmap" table in `docs/market/summary.md` are treated as accepted product direction. Where they change the spec, the REQ cites "summary R-n" and the MA-ID, and the change is listed under [Deviations from spec](#deviations-from-spec). The spec itself is not edited.

## How to read this document

**Personas** (spec §3.1): **DEV** Developer / Engineer · **TM** Technology Manager · **BA** Business Analyst / Data user · **BU** Business user · **OPS** Frontline / Operations user · **ASR** Assurance user · **DA** Department Admin · **PA** Platform Admin · **ALL** every persona.

**Surfaces:** CLI · Desktop · Web (workspace UI in the browser) · Console (`/me`, `/dept`, `/admin`) · Service (server-side behaviour with no dedicated UI, applies to every surface).

**Phases** follow spec §13 (0 Foundations, 1 MVP, 2 Productivity & Web Scale, 3 Department Packs & Quality, 4 Enterprise Hardening & Editions), adjusted by G1 direction as listed in [Deviations](#deviations-from-spec).

**Pilot context** (summary R-2, R-3): lighthouse pilot = **Technology pack (IT Service Management + Release Management) at a Qatar telco**. **Revenue Assurance** is the committed second pack (Phase 2). Numeric targets marked *(proposed)* are product proposals for G2 confirmation; they are not taken from a market source.

**Governance keywords** used in acceptance criteria: *server-side policy* (decided at control plane or gateway, never trusted from the client), *audited* (an immutable AuditEvent per REQ-071), *approval-gated* (paused until a named human approves, REQ-062), *residency* (data and inference stay within the tenant's configured region/tier, REQ-028, REQ-096), *PII-masked* (REQ-029/REQ-036, Arabic + English).
